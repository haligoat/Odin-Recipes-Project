Lasagna Recipe

Image:

This crockpot recipe features a savory beef and tomato sauce simmering together for robust flavor. Home cooks love tweaking the dish, swapping cheeses and adding sausage, to match their preference. Allrecipes member Cathy explains, "I will never do another Lasagna in the oven."

Ingredients:
  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 2 teaspoons minced garlic
  • (29 ounce) can tomato sauce
  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 16 ounces shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 12 ounces cottage cheese
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 (12 ounce) package lasagna noodles
Instructions:
  1. Make the meat sauce Cook ground beef (or sausage) in a pan until browned. Add onion, garlic, and tomato sauce, then simmer for 15–20 minutes. Prepare the cheese mixture
  2. In a bowl, mix ricotta cheese, an egg, salt, pepper, and a bit of parmesan until smooth. Cook the noodles
  3. Boil lasagna noodles until al dente (or use no-boil noodles if preferred). Layer everything
  4. Make the meat sauce Cook ground beef (or sausage) in a pan until browned. Add onion, garlic, and tomato sauce, then simmer for 15–20 minutes. Prepare the cheese mixture
  5. In a baking dish, layer sauce → noodles → ricotta mix → mozzarella. Repeat until full, finishing with sauce and cheese on top. Bake and rest Bake at 375°F (190°C) for about 30–40 minutes until bubbly and golden. Let it rest 10–15 minutes before cutting so it holds together.
